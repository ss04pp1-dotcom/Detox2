# r9.4 (v2.5.4 / versionCode 19) — study breaks + subjects, and a reboot timing bug

Item 2 of the audit's priority list. Built on r9.3.

## Fixed (important): a reboot silently stretched sessions, cages and temporary unlocks
Session, cage and temp-unlock timing is stored as `SystemClock.elapsedRealtime()`
(uptime). Uptime **restarts at zero on every boot**, but the persisted
`endElapsed` is an uptime from the *previous* boot. Read back after a reboot it
was compared with the new boot's tiny uptime, so:
- a session lasted for as long as the previous uptime at start (hours, or
  days on a phone that had been up for days);
- a cage did the same;
- a temporary unlock outlived the reboot (a free unlock).
Every persisted timeline is now also stamped with its wall-clock end, the
boot count and the uptime at save time (`StateRepository`). On the first read
after a reboot: a session is rebased to its remaining wall-clock time; a cage
keeps its remaining wall-clock time; a temporary unlock is dropped; a study
break ends. Sessions written by an older build are stamped once on first read.
(Uses `Settings.Global.BOOT_COUNT`, plus an uptime-went-backwards check as a
fallback.)

## New: study break (pause / resume) — Social Sentry Focus-Mode parity
- New status `PAUSED` (deliberately NOT "enforcing": policy, accessibility,
  guards and the wall all stand down during a break; a paused session still
  counts as a live session, so nothing can start over it).
- Bounded so it cannot become a free exit: **Study mode only**, at most **3
  breaks per session**, each **5 or 10 minutes**, never during a cage /
  temporary unlock / Prime commit, not in the last 2 minutes, and every break
  auto-ends by the clock (alarm + sweep) — RESUME NOW ends it early.
- Break time is **added** to the session end (Social Sentry excludes paused
  time from the duration); focus stats (`focusSeconds`, XP minutes, history)
  exclude break time.
- A paused session can still be ended through the coin bailout.
- UI: "TAKE A BREAK" on the active Study screen; a dedicated break screen with
  a countdown and RESUME NOW; notification text while on a break.

## New: subjects + per-subject time
- Optional subject per Study session (chips on the Study setup screen; "+ Add",
  long-press to delete; max 12 subjects, 24 characters each).
- Focus time (breaks excluded) is added to the subject on completion or
  bailout; "TODAY BY SUBJECT" is shown on the setup screen.
- Study day = date of (now - 4 h), i.e. a session past midnight counts toward
  the day you started it (Social Sentry's 04:00 boundary). Stored in a small
  SharedPreferences store (`StudySubjectStore`), 45 days kept — reporting only,
  it never affects enforcement.

## Bridge
`pauseSession`, `resumeSession`, `getStudySubjects`, `addStudySubject`,
`removeStudySubject`; `startSession` accepts `subject`. The session JSON gained
`paused`, `pauseRemainingSeconds`, `pauseCount`, `maxPauses`,
`pausedTotalSeconds`, `subjectName`.
